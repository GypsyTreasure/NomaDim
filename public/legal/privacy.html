Privacy Policy

NomaDim by NomaDirection · GDPR (EU 2016/679) · last updated 2026-08-07

NomaDim is privacy-first: the application runs entirely in your browser and your design files never leave your device. This policy explains what limited personal data is processed around the product, on what legal basis, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Data controller

The controller is NomaDirection (Poland) — [registered business name, address and tax number: to be completed by the owner before launch]. Contact: kontakt@nomadirection.pl. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as our processing does not require one; the contact address above handles all privacy requests.

What we process, why, and on what legal basis

  • The application — none. No account, no tracking, no cookies, no model uploads. Your documents live in your browser’s local storage and in files you download. A Pro license key is verified offline in your browser; the key is stored locally and is not transmitted to us.
  • Marketing site — aggregate analytics. The marketing pages may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (e.g. Plausible) that record aggregate page views only — no personal data, no cross-site tracking, no consent banner required. Legal basis: our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in understanding aggregate traffic.
  • Purchases — handled by our Merchant of Record. When you buy Pro, our Merchant of Record (MoR, e.g. Paddle or Lemon Squeezy) collects your email and payment details under their privacy policy, acting as an independent controller and as the seller of record for tax purposes. They deliver your license key and issue the invoice. We receive only what is needed to provide support and honour the licence (such as your email and an order id). Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in fraud prevention and record-keeping.
  • Optional crash reports — only with your consent. Crash reporting is off by default. If you switch it on in Settings, anonymised, PII-scrubbed diagnostic reports (error type, stack with file paths reduced to basenames, browser/OS version — never your design data or any text you typed) are sent to our processor (Sentry). Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by switching the setting off.

Recipients & processors

We share personal data only with: our Merchant of Record (payments, invoicing, refunds); our error-reporting processor (Sentry) if you opt in; and our static hosting provider (GitHub Pages) which may log standard technical request data. We do not sell personal data.

International transfers

Some processors (e.g. the MoR or Sentry) may process data outside the EU/EEA. Where they do, transfers are covered by an adequacy decision or by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, as set out in each provider’s data-processing terms.

Retention

We keep purchase and support records only as long as necessary to provide support, honour the licence, and meet legal/accounting obligations (in Poland, tax records are generally kept for 5 years). Opt-in crash reports are retained by our processor for a limited diagnostic window and then deleted.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). Because the app itself collects nothing, most requests concern purchase records — contact kontakt@nomadirection.pl and, where the data is held by the Merchant of Record, we will direct you to them.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the data protection authority in your EU country of residence.

Children

NomaDim is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 16.
