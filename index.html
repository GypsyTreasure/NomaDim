Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. NomaDim runs entirely in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). You can also install it as an app from your browser for offline use.

Where are my files stored?

On your device. Models are saved as local .nomadim.xml files and autosaved in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Is the Pro license a subscription?

No — Pro is a one-time purchase (a perpetual license). Buy once, use forever.

What can I import and export?

Import SVG, DXF and STEP; export STL (binary/ASCII) and STEP. 3MF export is on the roadmap.
