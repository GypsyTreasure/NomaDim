Right of withdrawal (EU consumers)

If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA, you normally have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases under the Consumer Rights Directive (2011/83/EU). NomaDim Pro is digital content supplied without a tangible medium. At checkout you are asked to expressly consent to immediate supply of the licence key and to acknowledge that you therefore lose the statutory right of withdrawal once supply begins (i.e. once the key is delivered/downloaded), as the Directive permits. Until you give that consent and delivery begins, the 14-day right applies.

Our voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee

On top of your statutory rights — and even after the key has been delivered — if NomaDim Pro is not right for you, request a refund within 14 days of purchase for a full refund, no questions asked. This voluntary guarantee is our commitment beyond what the law requires.

Business customers (B2B)

The statutory consumer right of withdrawal does not apply to purchases made by businesses. Our voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee above is still offered to business customers at our discretion.

Faulty or non-conforming software

Separately from the above, if the Pro features do not conform to what was described, EU consumers have statutory remedies under the Digital Content Directive (2019/770) — see the License Agreement. These apply regardless of the 14-day windows.

How to request

Refunds are processed by our Merchant of Record (who handled your purchase and any applicable VAT). Email kontakt@nomadirection.pl with your order id and we will help; you may also contact the Merchant of Record directly using the details on your invoice.
