Privacy Policy

NomaDim by Kacper / NomaDirection · last updated 2026-07-30

NomaDim is privacy-first: the application runs locally in your browser and your design files never leave your device.

What we collect

The app: nothing. No account, no tracking, no cookies, no model uploads. Your documents are saved in your browser’s local storage and as files you download.

The marketing site: may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (e.g. Plausible) that records aggregate page views only — no personal data, no cross-site tracking, and no consent banner required.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our Merchant of Record, who processes your email and payment details under their own privacy policy to deliver your license key. We store no payment information.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because the app collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, export, or erase on our side. For purchase records, contact the Merchant of Record or us at kontakt@nomadirection.pl.
